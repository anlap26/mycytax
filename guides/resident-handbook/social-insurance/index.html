How to get a social insurance number in Cyprus

The Social Insurance Services issue the number when a person is gainfully occupied, as an employee or as self-employed. Arrival alone does not create one.

Short answer

The social insurance number is the number on the Social Insurance Services’ scheme. EURES Cyprus, a government page, says the scheme covers compulsorily every person gainfully occupied in Cyprus, whether employed or self-employed. Employed persons include civil servants and apprentices. A person who is not gainfully occupied is not brought in by the mere fact of a yellow slip. Voluntary insurance is a separate door, for someone who wants to continue after a period of compulsory insurance, or who works abroad in the service of a Cypriot employer.

Steps if you are employed

  1. The employer registers, if not already registered, in the employers’ register. Business in Cyprus says the form is the application for registration of an employer, filed at the district Social Insurance Office, and that hiring is then notified electronically through ERGANI.
  2. If you have no social insurance number, the employer files the application for registration of an employed person. The Ministry’s Single Digital Gateway page names form YKA 1-008, together with the employee hiring declaration, form YKA 1-003, sent to the district office.
  3. The number that comes back is the one the employer uses on the earnings record. You do not invent a number in the meantime.

Steps if you are self-employed

Business in Cyprus says the self-employed application, a sample of YKA 1-008, is filed by hand or by post at the district Social Insurance Office, with a copy of an identity card, a passport or an alien registration certificate. The same page says the number is usually given within one to two weeks. That timing is the portal’s, not a guarantee printed as a statutory limit.

Contributions the official pages state

EURES Cyprus sets the rates by contribution year. For a contribution year beginning on or after 1 January 2024, and before the next step on 1 January 2029, it states 22.8 per cent for an employee, of which 8.8 per cent is paid by the employee, 8.8 per cent by the employer and 5.2 per cent from the Consolidated Fund. For a self-employed person in the same window it states 21.8 per cent, of which 16.6 per cent is paid by the self-employed person and 5.2 per cent from the Consolidated Fund. Later steps, in 2029, 2034 and 2039, are on the same page. Use that page if your contribution year is not the 2024 window. Earnings are subject to a ceiling. This page does not print the ceiling, because the EURES extract used here states the rates and not the euro cap.

Documents

For an employee, the employer’s forms and your identity. For a self-employed person, YKA 1-008 and a copy of the identity card, passport or alien registration certificate, as Business in Cyprus states. District offices are listed on the Ministry page: Nicosia, Limassol, Larnaca, Paphos and Famagusta.

What the number is not

It is not the tax identification code. GESY can use the Social Insurance record as one of the registries that identify you, which is why employment and GESY often travel together. A visitor on a pink slip who is not allowed to work is not in this compulsory scheme by reason of the permit.

Questions

Can I apply before I have a job?

The compulsory scheme, as EURES states it, covers persons who are gainfully occupied. An employer registers an employee who has no number. A self-employed person files YKA 1-008. A person with neither employment nor self-employment is not given a number by this page’s sources merely for arriving.

Is the employee share 8.8 per cent?

EURES Cyprus states that split for a contribution year beginning on or after 1 January 2024: 8.8 per cent employee, 8.8 per cent employer, 5.2 per cent from the Consolidated Fund, inside a total of 22.8 per cent. Check the page if a later statutory step has started.

Does a pink slip include a social insurance number?

No. A visitor’s permit does not allow economic activity, and the social insurance number follows gainful occupation or the voluntary route the Services describe.
