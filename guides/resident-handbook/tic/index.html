How to get a Cyprus tax identification code

The Tax Department issues the number through Tax For All. You create an account, then register as a taxpayer, and you attach the documents on the Department’s own list.

Short answer

The tax identification code is the number on the Tax Department’s register. The Department’s English pages call it a TIN. You obtain it by registering online. The Business in Cyprus portal, a government site, says a request to join the tax register is submitted only through the Tax For All portal, and that the first step is an account on that portal. This page does not describe the shape of the number. The public guides describe the process and the attachments, not a pattern to memorise.

You need the number before you can file. From the 2026 tax year the individual return is filed only in Tax For All. Who must file is on the income-tax return page.

Steps

  1. Open Tax For All and create an account. The portal is run by the Tax Department. An account is the sign-in. It is not yet the taxpayer record.
  2. Follow the Department’s online registration guides. There is a user guide for an individual, a separate one for a self-employed person, and a separate one for a non-individual.
  3. Attach the documents in the file the same page publishes, “Required Documents for Online Registration for Individuals”. The list is not the same for every person. Use the PDF for your own case.
  4. Wait for the Department to accept the registration and to issue the TIN. The English guides page does not publish a processing time, so this page does not state one.
  5. If you already have a TIN from an earlier year, do not register again. The Tax For All document index includes material on access to an existing taxpayer. Start from that document index rather than from a second registration.

Documents

The Department’s PDF is the list. This page does not copy it, because a copied checklist goes stale and because the PDF already sorts the papers by category. Have the identity document the PDF asks for, and the residence evidence it asks for, before you start. An address, a telephone number and an email address are part of a registration the portal can actually reach you on. Read the PDF for anything beyond that.

A VAT registration is a later step. The Business in Cyprus portal says you need a TIN before the VAT form, and that if the TIN is not issued yet you put the registration reference on the VAT form. That is a business step, not the first step for a person who only needs to file an individual return.

What this number is not

It is not the social insurance number. That comes from the Social Insurance Services, and only in the cases on the social insurance page. It is not the alien registration number on a residence card. It is not, by itself, a decision that you are tax resident. Residence is the 60-day or 183-day test.

Questions

Can I file a return without a TIN?

No. From the 2026 tax year the individual return is submitted only through Tax For All, and the register entry is what that system files against. Create the account, then the taxpayer registration, before the deadline that applies to you.

I have no income. Do I still register?

If you are a Cyprus tax resident and you are between 25 and 70 during the tax year, the Tax Department says you file even where you have no income. Filing needs a TIN. A resident with no gross income who is under 25, or over 70, is outside that filing rule. The detail is on the return page.

Is there a fee?

The registration guides page does not state a fee for joining the tax register. This page does not invent one. Any charge you are asked for should be the one shown inside Tax For All or on the Department’s current form.
