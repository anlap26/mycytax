Registering a car in Cyprus

A vehicle is cleared by Customs and then registered with the Road Transport Department before it can be licensed for road tax. A car already on the Cyprus register changes owner on form TOM 9B.

Short answer

There are two different jobs. One is putting a vehicle that is not yet on the Cyprus register onto it. Customs clears it, and the Road Transport Department registers it, and only then can it be licensed for road tax. The other is transferring a car that is already registered, from one keeper to the next, on form TOM 9B. The Department states a fee of €8.54 for that transfer. This page states that fee because the Department states it. It does not state a road-tax amount, because road tax depends on the vehicle and the Department’s current scale, which is not copied here.

A car you bring in

Customs says a vehicle imported from outside the European Union, new or used, is subject to import duty and VAT, assessed on a customs declaration with the invoice, the freight and any insurance to the point of entry. A used vehicle also needs the original foreign registration certificate. A new vehicle needs an EC certificate of conformity. After clearance the vehicle must be registered before it is licensed. Customs tells importers to ask the Road Transport Department, before they ship, whether that vehicle can be registered at all. Some vehicles cannot. The email the Customs page gives is roadtransport@rtd.mcw.gov.cy.

A vehicle from another EU member state is a different Customs path, including the question of relief. The Customs page on vehicles from member states is the one to read. This page does not summarise the relief conditions, because they are longer than a checklist and a wrong summary would be a false saving.

The Department’s registration page then splits by category. New category M1 cars, private saloons, are registered online by the importer, not by a private buyer walking in with a form. A used category M1 vehicle from a third country is also registered online by an authorised importer. A used vehicle from an EU member state has its own list: form TOM 6 signed by a technician at the district inspectorate, form TEL 72A from Customs where it is needed, the invoice or packing list, an MOT certificate where it applies, an emissions certificate on form TOM 119, the foreign registration certificate with a Press and Information Office translation unless it is in Greek or English, a road-tax disc from the old country where one is issued, SVA approval on form TOM 139B if the car is left-hand drive, and an insurance certificate. The page also mentions stamp duty of two times €1.71 on certain of those papers. Follow the page for the category you actually have. A category that is not M1 is a different list.

A car already registered in Cyprus

  1. Complete form TOM 9B for the current keeper and the new one.
  2. Take the registration certificate and the insurance certificate.
  3. Pay the fee of €8.54.
  4. Have the signatures witnessed. If both keepers are present, the clerk witnesses them against the identity cards. Otherwise a mayor or a certifying officer witnesses them.
  5. If there is no current MOT, the new keeper makes a statutory declaration on form TOM 120A that they know. Other cases, a taxi, a duty-free import, a deceased keeper, sit in the notes on the same page. Read the note that matches. Do not skip it because the ordinary case did not need it.

Documents

Identity for both keepers on a transfer. Insurance. The registration certificate. For an import, the Customs clearance and the Department’s category list, not a single bundle this page invents. A driving licence is a separate file. Registering the car does not exchange the licence.

Questions

Can I register a used EU car myself?

The Department’s page gives a document list for a used vehicle imported from an EU member state, including TOM 6, the foreign registration certificate and, for a left-hand-drive car, SVA approval. New M1 cars, and a used M1 car from a third country, are registered online by an importer. Read the row that matches the car.

What is the fee to transfer a Cyprus-registered car?

The Road Transport Department states €8.54, with form TOM 9B, the registration certificate and insurance.

Is road tax included?

No. The Department says a vehicle is registered before it can be licensed for road tax. This page does not print a road-tax figure.
