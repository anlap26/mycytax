Filing a personal income-tax return in Cyprus

From the 2026 tax year the return is filed only in Tax For All. The statutory deadline is 31 July of the next year, unless the Council of Ministers extends it. The 2025 return is due on 31 October 2026.

Short answer

The personal return is the individual income-tax return. The Tax Department still publishes a paper form T.D.1 for an employee, and it says to use paper only if the Department explicitly asks you to. Returns for 2017 to 2025 were filed only in TAXISnet. From the 2026 tax year the return is filed only in Tax For All. You need a tax identification code before that system has a record to file against.

Article 5 of the Assessment and Collection Law sets the deadline at 31 July of the following year. The Council of Ministers may extend it. For the 2025 tax year the Department says the deadline is 31 October 2026. For the 2026 tax year the statutory date is therefore 31 July 2027, unless an extension is published. This page will not treat a hoped-for extension as a date.

Who must file, from 2026

The Department’s page draws a line at the 2026 reform.

Before that reform, for tax years up to and including 2025, a Cyprus tax resident filed if gross annual income exceeded €19,500, and a non-resident filed if gross annual income from Cyprus exceeded €19,500.

From the 2026 tax year a Cyprus tax resident files if either of these is true. The person has gross income, from Cyprus or abroad, within section 5(1) of the Income Tax Law, at any age. Or the person is between 25 and 70 during the tax year, whatever the income, including none. A resident with no gross income who is under 25, or over 70, does not have to file. A non-resident files if there is income arising in Cyprus within section 5(2). The Council of Ministers may, by order, exempt categories for a given year.

The same page sets the bands on taxable income from the 2026 tax year: nil to €22,000, 20 per cent from €22,001 to €32,000, 25 per cent to €42,000, 30 per cent to €72,000, and 35 per cent above €72,000. Taxable income is not the same as gross income. The filing test looks at gross income and at age. The bands look at income after the exemptions and deductions the law allows. The tax residency guide explains who is inside the worldwide-income rule. The rental calculator applies these bands to rent.

Steps

  1. Register for a TIN if you do not have one. The return page assumes you are on the register.
  2. For a 2025 return, use TAXISnet. The Department’s deadline for that year is 31 October 2026. Do not wait for Tax For All: the Department says Tax For All is the route from the 2026 tax year.
  3. For a 2026 return, use Tax For All, by 31 July 2027 unless the deadline is extended by decree.
  4. If you find an error after the deadline, the Department says to file the amended-return form with your district office. It publishes the form on the same page.
  5. Keep the assessment that comes back. The Department notes that returns have been cleared up to and including 2023, and that 2024 and 2025 were still in progress when the page was read.

Documents

The electronic return is the filing. What you need in front of you is the material the questions ask for: employment certificates, evidence of rent, foreign income that section 5(1) reaches if you are resident, and the TIN. The Department does not, on the return page, publish one universal bundle of uploads. This page does not invent one. If a field asks for a figure you cannot support, leave it until you can.

What the return is not

It is not the provisional-tax computation that self-employed people meet during the year. This page does not state those instalment dates, because they are not on the individual-return page that is the source here. It is not capital-gains clearance on a sale of land. That is the selling guide. It does not, by being filed, decide the 60-day rule. You still have to meet the rule. The return is where the result is declared.

Keeping the papers the return needs

The return itself is filed in Tax For All. The receipts, payslips and invoices still have to sit somewhere until you file. The buttons are searches on Amazon.de for a kind of product, not a particular model. Stock and price are whatever Amazon shows that day. These are partner links.

Questions

When is the 2026 return due?

The Tax Department says Article 5 sets 31 July of the following year, so 31 July 2027 for the 2026 tax year, unless the Council of Ministers extends it. The published extension the page states is for 2025: 31 October 2026.

I am 30, tax resident, and had no income. Do I file?

Yes, on the Department’s wording. From 2026 a resident between 25 and 70 files even with no income. A resident with no gross income who is under 25 or over 70 does not.

Is T.D.1 still the form?

The Department still publishes a paper T.D.1 and says to use paper only when it explicitly asks. From the 2026 tax year the submission channel is Tax For All, not a paper T.D.1 sent on your own initiative.
