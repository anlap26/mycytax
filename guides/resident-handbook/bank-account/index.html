Opening a bank account in Cyprus

A consumer who legally resides in the European Union has a right of access to a payment account with basic features. A mortgage, and the papers a particular bank asks for, are a separate decision. This page does not invent a document list.

Short answer

The Central Bank of Cyprus publishes a leaflet on payment accounts with basic features. A consumer who legally resides in the European Union has a right of access to such an account, irrespective of place of residence. The leaflet says persons without a permanent address, asylum seekers, and persons without a residence permit whose removal is impossible in practice or in law, are not deprived of that access. Access does not depend on the consumer’s financial position. The account is for personal use, not for a business. Salary paid into it still counts as personal use. It can be a joint account. Opening it does not oblige you to buy insurance or any other product.

The fees for the services are set by decrees of the Minister of Finance, published on the Ministry’s website. The leaflet does not print a tariff. This page does not invent one. The account is covered by the deposit-guarantee scheme. The leaflet states the maximum compensation at €100,000 per depositor per licensed credit institution, including interest, across the depositor’s deposits at that institution. The list of institutions that offer the account is on the Central Bank’s website, in the material on the directive about fee comparability, switching and access.

Steps

  1. Read the Central Bank leaflet. It is the description of the right, not a form.
  2. Use the Central Bank’s list to see which credit institutions provide the account.
  3. Apply to one of them. The leaflet does not publish one set of documents for every bank. Each institution still has to identify its customer. Ask that institution for its current list. This page will not paste a private checklist of utility bills, tax numbers and reference letters and present it as law.
  4. If the account you want is a mortgage, or a facility for a company, you are outside the basic-account right. The bank decides. The Central Bank’s loan-to-value ceilings are on the mortgage page. They are ceilings, not an offer.

Documents

Whatever the bank’s own list says, have the identity document you will actually be asked to show, and the residence evidence you already hold: a yellow slip, a pink slip or another permit if you are not a Cypriot. A tax identification code is often requested in practice. The leaflet does not make it a statutory attachment of the basic account, so this page does not say the application fails without one.

What the right is not

It is not a right to a loan. It is not a right to an account denominated and priced however you prefer. It is not a substitute for the bank’s refusal where the law allows a refusal. The leaflet is the place to read the limits. The deposit guarantee of €100,000 is not advice to hold more than that at one institution.

Questions

Does every new resident get a current account?

The right the Central Bank describes is access to a payment account with basic features, for a consumer legally residing in the European Union. It is not a promise that every commercial package will be opened.

Are my deposits guaranteed without limit?

No. The leaflet states €100,000 per depositor per licensed credit institution, including accrued interest, across deposits at that institution.

Can the basic account pay the electricity bill?

The leaflet lists direct debits, including utility bills, among the services. Whether a particular utility’s direct debit is offered is a question for that utility and that bank.
