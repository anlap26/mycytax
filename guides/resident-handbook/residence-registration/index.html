Yellow slip (MEU1) and pink slip in Cyprus

The yellow slip is the MEU1 registration certificate for an EU, EEA or Swiss citizen. The pink slip is a visitor’s temporary residence permit, form MVIS8, for a third-country national. They are not the same application.

Short answer

People say “yellow slip” for the registration certificate issued to a citizen of the European Union, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, form MEU1. People say “pink slip” for a visitor’s temporary residence permit issued to a third-country national, form MVIS8. The Migration Department publishes them as two procedures, with two fees and two different rights. A yellow slip is not permission for a third-country national, and a pink slip does not let its holder work.

Neither document is tax residence. That test is on the tax residency guide. Neither is the immigration permit under Regulation 6(2). The residence options overview sets the categories next to each other.

Yellow slip: who and by when

A Union citizen, and a family member who is also a Union citizen, may stay for up to three months with a valid identity card or passport and with no further formality. For longer than three months the right exists if the person is a worker or self-employed, or has sufficient resources for themselves and their family so as not to become a burden on social assistance, and has comprehensive sickness insurance in Cyprus.

The application for the registration certificate is made within four months of entry. The fee is €20. If the file is complete, the Department says the decision is issued within one month at the latest. The certificate does not expire. It is sent by post if it is approved. A photograph is taken during the application, after you present the submission receipt and a valid travel document. Union citizens and their family members have free access to employment.

Failing to apply can bring a financial penalty of up to €2,500. The Department’s page names the offices and says whether an appointment is needed. Use that page, not an old photocopy.

Yellow slip: documents

The MEU1 application must be accompanied by the supporting documents on the Department’s list. Documents must be officially translated and duly certified. This page does not reprint that list. An older public checklist still asks applicants to bring passport photographs. The Department’s current MEU1 page says the photograph is captured at the application. Follow the current list.

The Ministry of Interior’s residence-card note adds one later step. An EU citizen who has resided legally for five consecutive years acquires a right of permanent residence if the requirements are met. The permanent card is issued on payment of €20. That is form MEU3, not a renewal of the yellow slip, because the yellow slip has no expiry date.

Pink slip: what it is

Under Aliens and Immigration Regulation 14, a visitor’s permit is for a third-country national who enters for a short or long holiday, for touring, or to look into the possibility of residing. The holder cannot carry out an economic activity. The Department’s categories include an autonomous visitor, a dependent spouse, a dependent minor child, certain clergy, a dependent adult family member of a person who holds an immigration permit, and a dependent partner of a Cypriot citizen in a proven relationship of at least a year, whose permit is issued for three months so that the marriage can take place. Other family relationships use other forms. Do not force a case onto MVIS8 if the Department has given it another page.

Entry is through a legal point, with a visa issued by a consular authority of the Republic and a valid travel document. The application is filed before the visa expires.

Pink slip: steps and fees

  1. Complete form MVIS8 and the supporting documents for that category. Documents issued abroad must be officially translated and duly certified. The list sits with the form. This page does not copy a private checklist of bank guarantees, medical tests or deposit amounts, because those figures are not on the Department’s procedure page.
  2. File at the application offices. Check on the Department’s page whether you need an appointment.
  3. Pay the fee. Issue or renewal of the residence permit is €70. First registration in the Aliens’ Register, which produces the alien registration card, is another €70, and only if you are not already registered.
  4. Give biometrics. A photograph, fingerprints and a signature are taken against the submission receipt and a valid travel document. A child who has not reached six years gives a photograph only. The same capture is done on a renewal.
  5. If the file is approved, a letter explains how to collect the permit. If it is refused, a letter gives the reasons.

The Department says examination is usually completed within four months if the documents are sufficient. The first visitor permit is for one year, unless the passport expires sooner, in which case the permit stops three months before the passport does. Clergy of the Orthodox Church or of the churches of the religious minorities can be issued for three years where the church asks and the papers are produced. Dependants are limited to the permit of the person they depend on. The permits are renewable if the criteria are still met. The three-month marriage category is not a one-year permit.

What to read next

A person who will work as a third-country national is in a different procedure, for a residence and employment permit, not in the visitor category. A person who will invest under Regulation 6(2) uses that guide. GESY enrolment, for a non-Cypriot, expects a valid residence permit. That step is the GESY page.

Questions

Does the yellow slip expire?

The Migration Department says registration certificates do not have an expiration date. A permanent-residence card after five years of legal residence is a separate document, form MEU3, and the Ministry states a €20 fee for it.

Can I work on a pink slip?

No. The Department says the holder of a visitor’s permit cannot carry out an economic activity in the Republic. Employment is a different permit.

Is either card tax residence?

No. Tax residence is the 60-day rule or the 183-day rule. A yellow slip or a pink slip can be part of the facts of a life in Cyprus. It is not the tax test.
