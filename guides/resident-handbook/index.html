This handbook answers the practical questions that come after a decision to live in Cyprus. Each page gives the short answer first, then the steps and the documents the authority itself describes, and it links that page. Where an official source does not publish a fee, a deadline or a processing time, the handbook says so. It does not fill the gap with a typical figure.

Where this sits next to the other guides

A registration certificate is not tax residence. The 60-day and 183-day rules are a different test, and non-dom status is a further one. An immigration permit under Regulation 6(2) is not a yellow slip and not a pink slip. The relocation guides put the first months in one order.

Questions

Do I need every page?

No. An EU citizen who will work starts with the yellow slip, the tax number and, once there is a job or a self-employed registration, social insurance. A third-country visitor who will not work starts with the pink slip. Social insurance is for people who are gainfully occupied, not for everyone who arrives. Read the page that matches the question.

Is the handbook tax or immigration advice?

No. The steps are taken from the official pages linked on each article. They are general information. Rules change, and a file can fall outside the ordinary case. Check the linked source, and take advice on your own facts, before you rely on a result.
