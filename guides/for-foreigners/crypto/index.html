Crypto tax in Cyprus from 2026

Article 20E taxes a gain on disposing of a crypto-asset at 8% from 1 January 2026. Mining is at the ordinary rates. An unrealised rise is not taxed.

Short answer

Article 20E of the Income Tax Law, in the CyLaw consolidation, says the profits of any person from disposing of crypto-assets are taxed at 8%. The Tax Department’s questions and answers, on disposals from 1 January 2026, say the same and add the edges. Mining is not in that 8%. A rise in market price while the asset is still held is, in the Department’s words, unrealised and not taxed.

Keeping the keys is a different page. The storage guide does not choose a share of savings and it is not a tax assessment.

Who, and from which day

The Department says the article applies to disposals from 1 January 2026, whatever the date of acquisition. There is no election: the article applies to every disposal inside its field.

For an individual the field is a Cyprus tax resident, or a non-resident who disposes of the asset while physically in Cyprus. For a company it is a tax resident, or a non-resident disposing through a permanent establishment in Cyprus. For the non-resident cases the Department says the applicable treaty, if there is one, is taken into account. The treaty list is a separate page. This page did not copy source articles on crypto out of those texts.

The Department says the treatment of individuals and of companies is the same.

What a disposal is, and what an asset is

The statute defines a disposal as a sale, a gift, an exchange of one crypto-asset for another, and the use of a crypto-asset as a means of payment.

The statute sends the word “crypto-asset” to point 5 of paragraph 1 of Article 3 of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114. The Department lists cryptocurrency, utility tokens, asset-referenced tokens and e-money tokens. As a general rule, the Department writes, an NFT is not in the term. A gain on a transaction that does not fall in Article 20E is taxed under the ordinary provisions of the law.

Where the same asset was bought at different times and at different prices, the Department treats the earlier purchase as the one disposed of. That is FIFO.

Mining, a loss, and what is not stated

A gain on disposing of an asset acquired by mining is, in the Department’s answers, taxed at the ordinary income-tax rates, not at 8%. Paragraph 4 of Article 20E says the article does not apply to a disposal of an asset acquired through mining.

A loss on a disposal may, under the statute, be set only against a gain on disposing of crypto-assets in the same tax year. A carry-forward, and a surrender to another company under Article 13, is not allowed.

This site has no calculator for the 8% rate: the multiplication is short, and a calculator would not decide whether a particular trade falls in the article. Staking rewards are not, in the part of the Department’s questions that was read, separated from mining in one sentence that could be narrowed to a rate. Where a receipt is not a disposal under Article 20E, the Department points to the ordinary rules. This page is not asked to guess a staking rate.

Questions

Is 8% the rate for a company as well?

The Department says the treatment of individuals and of companies is the same. Article 20E speaks of the profits of any person from a disposal.

Is a price rise taxed while the coin is unsold?

No. The Department calls that movement unrealised and says it is not taxed.

Is a mined coin also at 8%?

No. Both the statute and the Department’s answers take a disposal of an asset acquired by mining out of Article 20E. It is taxed at the ordinary rates.

Does a loss carry into the next year?

Article 20E allows a set-off only against a gain on disposing of crypto-assets in the same year. A carry-forward is expressly excluded.
