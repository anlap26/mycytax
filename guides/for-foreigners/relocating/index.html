Guides · Cyprus tax for foreigners in 2026

Taxes when you relocate to Cyprus

The 183-day rule and the 60-day rule as the Tax Department states them, the 2026 bands, and what non-dom status does not remove from a salary.

Figures on this site are taken from the official and primary sources linked on each page and describe the position as published for 2026. They are general information, not legal, tax or investment advice, and not a quote from the Land Registry or the Tax Department. Rules, reliefs and local fees change, and a transaction can fall outside the ordinary case. Check the linked source, and take advice on your own facts, before you rely on a result.

Last checked: 26 November 2026.

Short answer

Moving is not tax residence. The Tax Department’s residency page says an individual is a Cyprus tax resident if the 183-day rule or the 60-day rule in Article 2 of the Income Tax Law of 2002 is met. Until you are resident, Cyprus income tax looks at Cyprus-source income, not at your whole life abroad. Once you are resident, the statute charges the income Article 5 charges on a resident, including income outside Cyprus where that article says so. Exemptions are separate provisions. This page does not restate them, because the conditions are easy to overstate.

The 183-day rule

The Department says a person is treated as residing in Cyprus if they stay for one or more periods exceeding 183 days in total in a tax year. The day of arrival counts as a day in Cyprus. The day of departure counts as a day outside Cyprus. Arrival and departure on the same day count as a day in Cyprus. Departure and arrival on the same day count as a day outside Cyprus.

The 60-day rule

The Department says a person can be tax resident without 183 days if every condition is met. They stay in Cyprus for at least 60 days in the tax year. They do not stay more than 183 days in another country. They carry on a business in Cyprus and/or are employed in Cyprus and/or hold an office. They maintain a permanent home in Cyprus, owned or rented. If the business or the employment stops during the year, the person ceases to be a Cyprus tax resident for that year.

The Department’s page, read on 26 November 2026, still contains the condition about 183 days in another country. Some commentaries say the old words “not tax resident anywhere else” were removed from the rule. This page follows the Department’s text, not a commentary.

The day count and the papers are in the tax residency guide. The first months of living here, which are not a tax period, are in the note on the first months.

The bands, once income is in income tax

Business in Cyprus publishes the bands from the 2026 tax year: 0% on €0–€22,000, 20% on €22,001–€32,000, 25% on €32,001–€42,000, 30% on €42,001–€72,000 and 35% on €72,001 and above. The site calls the nil band an increase from €19,500 to €22,000.

The same site says who files from the 2026 tax year. A tax resident files if they have gross income, in or outside Cyprus, that falls under Article 5(1), whatever their age, and/or if they are aged 25 to 70 in the tax year, even with no income. A non-resident files if they have Cyprus income under Article 5(2). From 2026 the individual return is filed in Tax For All. The number is in the tax-code guide.

What non-dom does not do here

Non-dom status takes dividends and interest out of Special Defence Contribution. It does not change the income-tax bands above. The contribution on rent was abolished for 2026 for a domiciled person as well, so non-dom no longer explains rent. The two columns are in the calculator. The full conditions, including 17 years out of 20 and the €250,000 payment, are in the non-dom guide.

This page does not state social-insurance or GHS percentages. A 2026 ceiling from the GHS law is not reproduced here. Getting the number is a separate page.

An order that follows the rules

  1. Decide the immigration step from the passport. The map is the residence options page. The digital nomad visa is a separate page, and it does not replace the day count.
  2. Keep arrival and departure dates the way the Department counts them, and do not mix them with the visa.
  3. If a return will be due, get the tax number before you file.
  4. Before a dividend is paid, answer domicile separately. It is not the same answer as residence.

Questions

Do the first 90 days exempt the tax?

No. The Department does not describe that period in the Income Tax Law. Residence is counted on the tax year.

Does the 60-day rule work if I am tax resident somewhere else?

The Department’s page requires that you do not spend more than 183 days in another country, plus work or an office, plus a home. This page does not copy the sentence “not tax resident anywhere else” from a commentary.

From what amount does tax start in 2026?

Business in Cyprus states 0% on the first €22,000 of taxable income. That is income after the exemptions and deductions the statute allows, not necessarily salary before deductions.

Must I file if there is no income?

The same government site says a tax resident aged 25 to 70 on 31 December files even with no income. The exception is a person under 25 or over 70 with no gross income.
