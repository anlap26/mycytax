A foreigner buying property: VAT, transfer fees and stamp duty

The two ways a 2026 purchase is taxed. Stamp duty on a new contract is €0. Legal fees and the agent are not estimated here.

Figures on this site are taken from the official and primary sources linked on each page and describe the position as published for 2026. They are general information, not legal, tax or investment advice, and not a quote from the Land Registry or the Tax Department. Rules, reliefs and local fees change, and a transaction can fall outside the ordinary case. Check the linked source, and take advice on your own facts, before you rely on a result.

Last checked: 26 November 2026.

Short answer

A purchase in Cyprus meets tax in one of two ways. If the transfer is a VAT transaction and the certificate is produced, Gov.cy says the Land Registry transfer fee is not charged. If there is no VAT, the fee follows the scale of 3%, 5% and 8%, and the legislation then exempts half, unless the sale was a forced sale under Parts VI and VIA of Law 9/1965. Stamp duty on a contract that at least one party signs on or after 1 January 2026 is nil.

A lawyer’s fee, an agent’s commission and local charges are not filled in here. They are not on one government table.

VAT

The standard rate is 19%. The 5% rate for a primary and permanent home does not cover the whole price. Since the amendment in force from 2023, 5% is on the first 130 square metres and up to €350,000, provided the whole area is not over 190 square metres and the whole value is not over €475,000. Outside those outer limits there is no 5%. The Tax Department has its own calculator. Use that before you file. The limits are set out on this site’s VAT calculator, which illustrates the rule and does not replace the Department’s tool.

Transfer fees

The Department of Lands and Surveys scale, before the 50% reduction, is 3% up to €85,000, 5% from €85,001 to €170,000 and 8% above that. The scale is applied to each buyer’s share. A contract not deposited within six months can, on the Department’s leaflet, increase the fee by 10% when title is transferred. The separate working is the transfer-fee calculator.

Stamp duty

Law 239(I)/2025 repealed stamp duty from 1 January 2026. A contract drawn up and signed by at least one party on or after that date is not stamped. A contract a party signed by 31 December 2025 stays on the old scale, capped at €20,000. That is the stamp-duty calculator.

The three lines together, without professional fees, are in the purchase-cost calculator.

A buyer from outside the EU

Before title is registered, that buyer needs the Ministry of Interior’s permission to acquire. The Ministry says the permission itself has no fee. The limits on what may be bought are on the Ministry’s page: up to 4,000 square metres of land for a home, or up to two units within the sizes the page states. The permission is not a residence permit. The guide to buying as a foreigner keeps them apart.

The rest of the taxes on a property, including gains tax at 20% on a sale and the fact that there is no annual state property tax, are in the property-tax overview. Local rates are still not guessed on this page.

Questions

Do I pay both VAT and transfer fees?

Gov.cy describes an exemption from the fees when the transfer is a VAT transaction and the certificate is produced. Where there is no VAT, the 50% reduction applies unless it was a forced sale. The purchase-cost calculator uses one of those two outcomes.

Is stamp duty still charged in 2026?

On a contract a party signs on or after 1 January 2026 the duty is €0. The old scale remains for a document a party signed by 31 December 2025.

Does the 5% rate cover the whole flat?

No. It covers the qualifying portion within 130 square metres and €350,000, and only if the whole property is inside 190 square metres and €475,000. Otherwise VAT is 19%.

What does the non-EU acquisition permission cost?

The Ministry of Interior says there is no fee for the permission itself. The limits on area and on the number of units are on its page.
