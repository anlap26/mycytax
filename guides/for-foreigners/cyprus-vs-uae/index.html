Cyprus and the United Arab Emirates: tax in 2026

The UAE does not levy personal income tax. Corporate tax is 0% up to AED 375,000 and 9% above, on the official government portal.

Figures on this site are taken from the official and primary sources linked on each page and describe the position as published for 2026. They are general information, not legal, tax or investment advice, and not a quote from the Land Registry or the Tax Department. Rules, reliefs and local fees change, and a transaction can fall outside the ordinary case. Check the linked source, and take advice on your own facts, before you rely on a result.

Last checked: 26 November 2026.

Short answer

The UAE government portal, dated 28 October 2026, says the UAE does not levy income tax on individuals. Corporate tax is 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% above that. Cyprus taxes a resident’s income on the 2026 bands and taxes a company at 15%.

Personal income tax

Cyprus, from the 2026 tax year, as published by Business in Cyprus: 0% on the first €22,000 of taxable income, 20% from €22,001 to €32,000, 25% to €42,000, 30% to €72,000 and 35% above that.

There is no personal income-tax scale to set beside the Cyprus bands. The portal states the absence of the tax. That is not a statement about every fee or indirect tax in the UAE.

Companies

The Tax Department’s company-return table puts corporate tax at 15% from 2026, and 12.5% on 2013–2025.

The same portal, citing the Ministry of Finance, states 0% up to AED 375,000 and 9% above. A qualifying free zone can be at 0% on qualifying income and 9% on other income. The conditions are not a single tick, so the free-zone rate is not applied as a general rate. This page does not convert dirhams to euros.

Dividends and interest

A person who is not domiciled in Cyprus is outside Special Defence Contribution on dividends and interest. A domiciled resident is generally at 5% on dividends from 2026 profits. A dividend from a Cyprus company out of profits up to the 2025 tax year stays at 17% if it is received by 31 December 2031. Interest of an individual is at 17%, or 3% where total income is under €12,000. The contribution on rent was abolished for 2026. Those points are on the Tax Department’s defence-contribution page and the Ministry of Finance incentives page.

Because the portal says there is no personal income tax, it does not publish a personal dividend or interest scale. A corporate distribution is inside the corporate-tax rules above, not a second personal rate this page invents.

Capital gains

Cyprus capital gains tax on immovable property in Cyprus is 20% of the gain after the official inflation adjustment, with the lifetime exemptions in the Capital Gains Tax Law. The capital-gains calculator is the working of that rule. It does not tax a gain on assets outside Cyprus.

No personal capital-gains scale was published on the pages read, consistent with the statement that there is no personal income tax. Corporate gains sit in the corporate tax.

Social insurance

No 2026 social-insurance rate for this country was copied from its social-insurance authority for this page. Cyprus GHS and social-insurance percentages are likewise not restated here. The social-insurance guide describes registration, not a rate.

Treaty with Cyprus

The list shows an agreement in force from 1 January 2014. The Ministry of Finance list was read on 26 November 2026. Withholding percentages in the treaty are not printed here, because the articles were not copied out of the gazette text. The treaty page collects the same list for the countries it already names. The comparison hub and the calculator use only the headline rates that page already ties to a source.

Residence and non-dom

Tax residence in Cyprus is the 183-day rule or the 60-day rule on the Tax Department’s residency page. Non-dom status is about domicile, not about this passport. It does not change the income-tax bands. The conditions are in the non-dom guide and on the relocation page.

Visa

A national of a country outside the European Union and the European Economic Area is not on the MEU1 route. The digital nomad scheme, on the Migration Department’s page posted 31 December 2025, asks for net income of at least €3,500 a month after contributions and tax, and the work must not be for an employer or a client in Cyprus. A visitor permit does not allow work. The digital nomad page has the ceiling of 500 permits and the family uplift. An employment permit is a different procedure, and this page does not restate its fee.

Who this comparison is for

A person who becomes Cyprus resident gives up a jurisdiction that, on the portal, does not tax personal income, and meets the Cyprus bands instead. A company at 9% above the dirham threshold is still below 15%, before any Cyprus tax on a dividend to a domiciled shareholder. The dirham threshold is not converted here.

What this page does not state

Figures that were not on an official 2026 page when this text was written are named in the sections above and are not used as a rate. A move does not, by itself, end tax in the country you leave. Exit taxes and reporting duties of that country were not copied onto this page unless a sentence above cites that authority.

Questions

Does the UAE tax a salary?

The government portal, dated 28 October 2026, says the UAE does not levy income tax on individuals.

What is the corporate rate?

0% up to AED 375,000 of taxable income and 9% above, on that portal. The free-zone conditions are not applied as a general exemption.

Why is there no euro figure for the threshold?

This page does not publish an exchange rate.

Is there a treaty?

Yes, in force from 1 January 2014. No withholding rate is stated.
