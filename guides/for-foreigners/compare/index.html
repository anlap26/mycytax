Cyprus, Malta, Portugal, the UAE and Greece: 2026 rates

The headline taxes official pages publish for 2026. Malta’s personal bands and Greece’s middle bands are not calculated.

Figures on this site are taken from the official and primary sources linked on each page and describe the position as published for 2026. They are general information, not legal, tax or investment advice, and not a quote from the Land Registry or the Tax Department. Rules, reliefs and local fees change, and a transaction can fall outside the ordinary case. Check the linked source, and take advice on your own facts, before you rely on a result.

Last checked: 26 November 2026.

Short answer

A country comparison on one salary is usually wrong once someone else’s deduction or social charge is filled in. Only headlines an authority has printed are stated here. The calculator applies the Cyprus 2026 bands, the Portugal 2026 mainland marginal scale and UAE personal income tax at nil. It does not calculate Malta’s personal scale for 2026: that scale was not on the rates page that was reviewed. It does not calculate the Greek 2026 personal scale either: the middle bands depend on the number of children, and the zero-child column did not read reliably out of the page text.

No country’s social insurance is in the calculator. The page does not publish a dirham rate.

Cyprus

Income tax from the 2026 tax year, Business in Cyprus: 0% up to €22,000, then 20%, 25%, 30% and 35% above €72,000. Corporate tax from 2026, Tax Department table: 15%. Defence contribution on a dividend is a separate company page. It is not part of the corporate 15%.

Malta

The Malta Tax and Customs Administration, on its corporate-tax page, says Maltese companies are taxed at 35% on worldwide income and capital gains. The company income-tax page, last updated on 8 October 2025, repeats a flat 35% on net chargeable income. The same corporate-tax page says that after a dividend the shareholder may be entitled to a refund of part or all of the tax the company paid. This page does not state the fraction of that refund, and the calculator does not apply one. An effective “5%” from other people’s summaries is not brought over.

Personal bands were found on an MTCA page for 2025. The 2026 legislative-notes page, as read, did not replace the rate. This site does not use the 2025 scale as if it were the 2026 scale. That figure is unverified for 2026, and it is not calculated.

Portugal

Lei n.º 64/2025, transitional rule: for periods beginning in 2026, the rate in paragraphs 1 and 5 of Article 87 of the IRC Code is 19%. The Portal das Finanças 2026 rate card puts 19% on the mainland. For the Azores and Madeira the card publishes reduced rates. The state-budget site states 16.8% for the Azores, while the tax portal’s card states 13.3%. Because two official pages differ, the calculator uses only the mainland 19% and does not calculate the autonomous regions.

Personal income tax, Decreto-Lei n.º 212/2026, the scale from 1 January 2026 on the mainland, marginal rates: 12.20% up to €8,342, 15.20% up to €12,587, 20.70% up to €17,838, 23.60% up to €23,089, 30.60% up to €29,397, 34.60% up to €43,090, 43.10% up to €46,566, 44.60% up to €86,634 and 48% above that. The calculator asks for rendimento coletável, income after deductions, not gross salary. Special regimes for new residents are not in the arithmetic.

United Arab Emirates

The official government portal, on a page dated 28 October 2026, says the UAE does not levy income tax on individuals. Corporate tax, on the same portal citing the Ministry of Finance: 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% above that. The calculator applies the nil band only to a euro amount you type yourself. No exchange rate is filled in. The free-zone rate of 0% on qualifying income is not in the calculator: the conditions on the FTA page do not collapse into one tick.

Greece

The Ministry of Economy and Finance guide: profits from business activity of legal persons, from tax year 2021 onwards, are at 22%, apart from the cases the guide names separately (29% for some banks, 10% for the agricultural cooperatives it names). The AADE return for a legal person also shows 22% among the rates on the tax line.

For an individual the guide publishes an employment and pension scale from 2026 that changes with the number of children, plus a tax reduction of €777 with no children, which shrinks by €20 per thousand euros of income above €12,000. The bands €30,001–€40,000 (34%), €40,000.01–€60,000 (39%) and above €60,000 (44%) are repeated on the page in every column that was printed. The bands between €10,000 and €30,000, in the text that was extracted, do not give a reliable column for a taxpayer with no children. The calculator does not apply that scale. It is read on the Ministry’s page.

The treaties of these countries with Cyprus, without withholding percentages, are on the treaty page.

Questions

Why is Malta’s personal tax not calculated?

The MTCA rates page that was read has the 2025 scales. A 2026 scale was not found in this check. The site does not put the old scale up as the new one.

Why can the UAE line charge 9% on the whole profit?

The nil band is expressed in dirhams. The page does not publish an exchange rate. Until you type a euro equivalent of AED 375,000, 9% is shown on the whole sum, and the line says so.

Is Portugal’s 19% the islands as well?

No. 19% is the mainland for 2026, in the statute and on the Portal das Finanças card. Island rates differ across two official pages, so the calculator does not use them.

What is the Greek rate on a salary with no children?

The Ministry publishes a scale that depends on children, and a €777 reduction. The middle bands, without a reliable zero-child column, are not reprinted here. The corporate 22% is printed in terms.
