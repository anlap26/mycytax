Guides · Cyprus tax for foreigners in 2026

Cyprus treaties: the UK, Russia, Ukraine, Israel, Germany, the US and India

What the Ministry of Finance list says about each of those states on 26 November 2026. This page does not print withholding rates.

Figures on this site are taken from the official and primary sources linked on each page and describe the position as published for 2026. They are general information, not legal, tax or investment advice, and not a quote from the Land Registry or the Tax Department. Rules, reliefs and local fees change, and a transaction can fall outside the ordinary case. Check the linked source, and take advice on your own facts, before you rely on a result.

Last checked: 26 November 2026.

Short answer

A treaty does not repeal Cyprus tax by itself. It divides taxing rights and can limit a withholding. The rate that counts is the rate in the text in force on the day of the payment, not a figure from someone else’s table. The Ministry of Finance keeps the list. The list page is marked 27 June 2024 and was read on 26 November 2026. The Tax Department points to that list and to the texts.

This page does not state dividend, interest or royalty percentages. The articles were not copied out of the Official Gazette texts. A rate table from somewhere else is not brought over.

United Kingdom

The list has a new agreement. Entry into force is given as 18 July 2018. An amending protocol is 2 October 2019. Earlier dates on the same row belong to the 1974 agreement and the 1980 protocol.

Russia

The list has an agreement in force from 17 August 1999, a protocol from 2 April 2012 and a further protocol from 15 January 2021. The page that was read does not record a suspension. Commentary outside the Ministry says Russia suspended part of the articles in 2023. This site did not read the decree and does not treat the suspension as a fact on the Cyprus side. While the Ministry keeps the row, the practical question goes to the text and to the Russian authority, not to a rate invented here.

Ukraine

The row is marked as a new agreement, in force from 19 August 2013, with a protocol from 28 November 2019. A footnote on the list says the treaty with the USSR is still in force for some states. Ukraine’s row records a new agreement. Which text applies to a particular payment is that text’s question, not this page’s.

Israel

Israel is not on the list that was read. No double-tax treaty was found on that Ministry page. Israel is on the list of jurisdictions for automatic exchange under the CRS. That is an exchange of information, not a division of tax.

Germany

The new agreement entered into force on 16 December 2011. An amending protocol is 8 December 2021. The row also keeps the dates of the 1974 agreement.

United States

The list has one row: signed 19 March 1984, in force 31 December 1985. Dividend, interest and royalty ceilings do not follow from that, because the articles of that text were not copied out. Do not treat the row as if it had already been checked as an income-tax treaty of the British or German kind. Open the Official Gazette text the Ministry links.

India

The new agreement entered into force on 14 December 2016. The row also has the earlier 1994 agreement.

Beside this, if the question is a country comparison

The same list includes Malta (in force from 11 August 1994), Portugal (from 16 August 2013), the United Arab Emirates (from 1 January 2014) and Greece (from 16 January 1969). Their domestic headline taxes are on the comparison page. Hong Kong was signed on 12 June 2026, and the entry-into-force cell on the list is empty. This page does not treat it as in force.

Residence in Cyprus, which a treaty claim usually needs, is on the relocation page.

Questions

Why is there no table of 0%, 5% and 15%?

Those figures travel in secondary summaries. The articles were not copied out for this page. The Ministry publishes the list and links to the texts. The rate is in the text.

Has the treaty with Russia been cancelled?

On the Ministry page as read on 26 November 2026 the Russia row is still there, with a protocol in force from 15 January 2021. A suspension is not recorded on it. This page neither confirms nor denies that suspension.

Is there an income-tax treaty with the United States?

There is one United States row, from 1984. Whether it covers dividends or only estates is not visible from the list. No ceilings are stated here.

Can an Israeli resident rely on a treaty?

Israel is not on the double-tax list. There is nothing to rely on until the Ministry adds a row. The CRS does not stand in for it.
