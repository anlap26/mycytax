Cyprus and Sweden: tax in 2026

Swedish state income tax at 20% above SEK 643,000 for 2026, municipal tax left local, and corporate tax at 20.6%, beside Cyprus.

Figures on this site are taken from the official and primary sources linked on each page and describe the position as published for 2026. They are general information, not legal, tax or investment advice, and not a quote from the Land Registry or the Tax Department. Rules, reliefs and local fees change, and a transaction can fall outside the ordinary case. Check the linked source, and take advice on your own facts, before you rely on a result.

Last checked: 26 November 2026.

Short answer

The Swedish Tax Agency’s 2026 amounts page charges no state income tax on taxable earned income up to SEK 643,000 and 20% above that. Municipal tax is charged as well and is set locally, so this page does not print a municipal percentage. Corporate tax is 20.6%. State tax on capital income is 30%.

Personal income tax

Cyprus, from the 2026 tax year, as published by Business in Cyprus: 0% on the first €22,000 of taxable income, 20% from €22,001 to €32,000, 25% to €42,000, 30% to €72,000 and 35% above that.

The threshold of SEK 643,000 is taxable earned income after the basic deduction. Before that deduction, the breakpoint is SEK 660,400 for someone who had not turned 66 at the start of the year, and SEK 760,500 for someone who had. There is no single national rate on a salary, because the municipal tax sits on top.

Companies

The Tax Department’s company-return table puts corporate tax at 15% from 2026, and 12.5% on 2013–2025.

The Agency’s company page states corporate tax at 20.6%. The 2026 amounts page states the same rate as state income tax on legal persons.

Dividends and interest

A person who is not domiciled in Cyprus is outside Special Defence Contribution on dividends and interest. A domiciled resident is generally at 5% on dividends from 2026 profits. A dividend from a Cyprus company out of profits up to the 2025 tax year stays at 17% if it is received by 31 December 2031. Interest of an individual is at 17%, or 3% where total income is under €12,000. The contribution on rent was abolished for 2026. Those points are on the Tax Department’s defence-contribution page and the Ministry of Finance incentives page.

State tax on capital income, which covers dividends and interest in that category, is 30% on the 2026 amounts page.

Capital gains

Cyprus capital gains tax on immovable property in Cyprus is 20% of the gain after the official inflation adjustment, with the lifetime exemptions in the Capital Gains Tax Law. The capital-gains calculator is the working of that rule. It does not tax a gain on assets outside Cyprus.

A gain that the Agency places in capital income is inside the 30% above. A separate property-gain computation was not copied.

Social insurance

No 2026 social-insurance rate for this country was copied from its social-insurance authority for this page. Cyprus GHS and social-insurance percentages are likewise not restated here. The social-insurance guide describes registration, not a rate.

Treaty with Cyprus

The list shows an agreement in force from 14 November 1989 and an amending protocol signed on 3 July 2026. The row, as read, does not give that protocol its own entry-into-force date. The Ministry of Finance list was read on 26 November 2026. Withholding percentages in the treaty are not printed here, because the articles were not copied out of the gazette text. The treaty page collects the same list for the countries it already names. The comparison hub and the calculator use only the headline rates that page already ties to a source.

Residence and non-dom

Tax residence in Cyprus is the 183-day rule or the 60-day rule on the Tax Department’s residency page. Non-dom status is about domicile, not about this passport. It does not change the income-tax bands. The conditions are in the non-dom guide and on the relocation page.

Visa

A citizen of a Union country who stays longer than three months applies for a registration certificate, form MEU1. The Migration Department’s page puts the fee at €20. The certificate is not tax residence and it is not non-dom status. The split is on the residence options page.

Who this comparison is for

A salary below the state-tax threshold still meets municipal tax, which this page will not invent. Company profit at 20.6% is above the Cyprus 15%. Capital income at 30% is the figure to set beside Cyprus defence contribution, which is nil for a non-domiciled resident and generally 5% on 2026 dividends for a domiciled one.

What this page does not state

Figures that were not on an official 2026 page when this text was written are named in the sections above and are not used as a rate. A move does not, by itself, end tax in the country you leave. Exit taxes and reporting duties of that country were not copied onto this page unless a sentence above cites that authority.

Questions

Is Swedish income tax 20%?

20% is the state tax above SEK 643,000 of taxable earned income. Below that threshold the state tax on earned income is nil, and municipal tax still applies. The municipal rate is not printed here.

What is the tax on Swedish dividends?

30% state tax on capital income, on the Agency’s 2026 amounts page.

Is the July 2026 protocol in force?

It is signed. The Ministry list, as read, does not show a separate entry-into-force date for it.

Does non-dom remove Swedish municipal tax?

No. Non-dom is a Cyprus status. It does not set the Swedish municipal rate, and this page does not say when Swedish residence ends.
