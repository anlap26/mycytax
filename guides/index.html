Yellow slip (MEU1) and pink slip in Cyprus

The yellow slip is the MEU1 registration certificate for an EU, EEA or Swiss citizen. The pink slip is a visitor’s temporary residence permit, form MVIS8, for a third-country national. They are not the same application.

Filing a personal income-tax return in Cyprus

From the 2026 tax year the return is filed only in Tax For All. The statutory deadline is 31 July of the next year, unless the Council of Ministers extends it. The 2025 return is due on 31 October 2026.

Exchanging a foreign driving licence in Cyprus

An EU licence may be exchanged after six months, if you choose. A licence from a country the Road Transport Department treats as equivalent must be exchanged after six months. The application is form TOM 7D, in person.
