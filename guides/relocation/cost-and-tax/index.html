Cost and tax when you move to Cyprus

Tax residence is the 60-day or 183-day rule. The 2026 bands start at €22,000. A move does not by itself create non-dom status, and this page does not invent a cost of living.

They are general information, not legal, tax or investment advice, and not a quote from the Land Registry or the Tax Department. Last checked: November 2026.

Short answer

Two different bills arrive with a move, and they should not be added together as if they were one. One is the cost of buying or renting a home. The other is income tax, once the Tax Department’s residence tests are met. This page points at the official tests and at the calculators. It does not print a monthly budget for Limassol or Paphos. No official source checked for this article publishes a single cost of living, and a made-up one would be the wrong kind of help.

Tax residence, not the residence card

You are tax resident if you are in Cyprus for more than 183 days in the calendar year, or if the 60-day rule is met in full: at least 60 days in Cyprus, not more than 183 days in any other one country, a business, employment or office in Cyprus that is not terminated during the year, and a permanent home you own or rent. The Tax Department’s current page lists those points. A yellow slip or a pink slip does not answer the test.

From the 2026 tax year the bands on taxable income are nil to €22,000, then 20, 25, 30 and 35 per cent at the thresholds on the return page. A resident aged 25 to 70 files even with no income. Filing needs a tax identification code.

Non-dom is a further status

Non-dom status is about domicile for Special Defence Contribution. It is for a person who is tax resident and not domiciled. It is not a discount on the income-tax bands, and it is not granted by the residence card. Read that guide before you repeat a sentence about dividends.

The cost of a home

On a purchase, use the total-cost guide and the calculators for transfer fees, VAT and stamp duty. Stamp duty is nil if no party signed before 1 January 2026. There is no state annual property tax from 2017. Local charges are local. If the comparison is with a home in Germany, Greece or Spain, the mortgage comparison sets the September 2026 average loan rate and the purchase taxes side by side, and it says where a single national figure does not exist.

Rent, if you let, is income. The rental calculator applies the 20 per cent statutory deduction, the 2026 bands and GHS at 2.65 per cent of gross rent for an individual, which is the Tax Department’s rental figure. It does not model a ceiling it has not copied from the current GHS tables.

What this page leaves out on purpose

School fees, a car’s road tax, a grocery basket and a typical electricity bill are not on an official page as one number for “moving to Cyprus”. The utilities page says how to put electricity and water in your name and refuses a typical deposit for the same reason. Take the supplier’s quote.

Questions

Does moving make me tax resident?

Only if the 183-day rule or the full 60-day rule is met. The residence card is a different file.

Are the old bands, starting at €19,500, still the ones to use?

For tax years up to and including 2025, the filing threshold the Department states was gross income above €19,500. From the 2026 tax year the nil band runs to €22,000, and the filing test is no longer that threshold.

Where is the cost of living?

It is not on this page. No single official figure was found, so none is printed. The purchase taxes and the loan-rate comparison are the figures that do have a source.
