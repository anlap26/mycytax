The first months after you arrive in Cyprus

A practical order for the first months. The legal clocks are the ones the departments publish: four months for a yellow slip, before the visa expires for a pink slip, not a homemade 30-60-90 timetable.

Short answer

There is no statute called “the first 90 days”. People search for one because the first months are crowded. The clocks that do exist are narrower. A Union citizen who will stay more than three months applies for the registration certificate within four months of entry. A third-country visitor applies for the temporary permit before the visa expires. Tax residence is counted across the calendar year, not across the first 90 days. Do the immigration step that matches your passport first. Then the tax number, if you will have to file. Then the services that need a name on an account.

An order that follows the rules

  1. Enter lawfully and keep the passport stamps. The yellow slip and pink slip page is the split. Book the Migration Department appointment early enough to meet the four-month MEU1 window, or the visa window for MVIS8. The Department does not promise a next-week slot.
  2. Put a roof over the stay that you can show: a lease or a title. Several later forms ask for it. The purchase itself, if there is one, is the foreign-buyer guide, not this list.
  3. If you will be gainfully occupied, the employer or your self-employed filing produces the social insurance number. If you will not work, do not wait for a number that the scheme does not issue for mere arrival.
  4. Register for a tax identification code once you know you must file. From 2026 a resident aged 25 to 70 files even with no income. The return page has the line.
  5. Open the payment account the Central Bank’s basic-account right describes, if a commercial account is not what you need. A mortgage is not that right.
  6. Enrol in GESY when the residence registry can identify you, and then choose the personal doctor. Until both steps are done, keep the insurance the Migration Department asked for.
  7. Transfer electricity and water into the name of the person who may occupy the home. A residence card is not a title and not a lease.
  8. If you will drive past the period your licence allows, start the exchange once the 185-day residence test is met. A car you import is a Customs and registration file of its own.

What not to compress

Do not file a tax-residence story before the days and the ties exist. The 60-day rule fails if the employment or the office ends during the year, and it fails if another single country takes more than 183 days. Do not treat the yellow slip as permission to skip the return. Do not treat the pink slip as permission to work.

Questions

Is day 90 a legal deadline?

Not as such. The Migration Department’s clock for a Union citizen is four months from entry for the registration certificate, after a right to stay three months on a passport or identity card alone. A visitor’s application is before the visa expires.

What if I am only buying and not moving?

The buying guide and the calculators are the right pages. This list is for a person who will live here. A purchase does not by itself start the yellow-slip clock or the tax-residence tests.
