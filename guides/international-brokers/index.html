Where to invest spare funds: review of international brokers for investors

What spare cash is for, what an international broker account does and does not cover, and where a bank transfer or a licensed money transfer service fits when you move currency. Interactive Brokers is the broker described. Not personal advice.

Spare money is not one pile, and a broker is not a savings account. This page is for someone in or around Cyprus who has already covered the near-term bills and is asking where a surplus can sit. It describes what an international broker account is for, what Interactive Brokers is as a type of firm, and where a bank transfer or a licensed money transfer service fits when the money is not yet in euros. It is a description, not a portfolio and not a personal recommendation.

Shares, bonds and funds can fall in value, including by more than any gain you hoped for. Cash you need for a completion, a tax payment or living costs does not belong in that price. Nothing here tells you to invest a particular sum.

Separate the money by when you will need it

A useful split is by date, not by product. Money you expect to send within the year, including the price and taxes on a purchase, stays where you can pay a lawyer without selling anything. Work the tax lines first. The transfer-fee calculator, the VAT calculator and the total-cost guide are the figures. The mortgage calculator turns a rate your bank has offered into a monthly payment. It does not say the payment is a good use of borrowing, and it does not say you should invest money you might need for the next instalment.

Money left after that, which you can leave untouched for years, is the only pile this page is about. Some people leave it in the bank. Some buy listed investments through a broker. Some already hold crypto, which is a different custody problem and is covered in the storage guide. Mixing the three into one “investment account” is how a house deposit ends up somewhere it cannot be sent on a Thursday.

Rent that arrives after tax is the same question on a smaller scale. The rental-yield calculator shows the tax drag on one year of rent. It is not a forecast of next year, and it is not an instruction to invest the surplus. A surplus you can afford to see fall is the only part that even raises the broker question.

What a broker account is

A broker account lets you buy and sell listed instruments: shares, bonds, funds and similar products the firm actually offers. The account can also hold cash in one or more currencies while you decide. That cash is not the same thing as a deposit at a Cyprus bank.

The Cyprus deposit-guarantee scheme, described by the Central Bank, covers eligible deposits at a member credit institution up to €100,000 per depositor per institution. A brokerage balance is not that deposit. If the firm is an EU investment firm, a different set of rules applies when the firm cannot return money or instruments it was holding for you. Directive 97/9/EC requires member states to provide cover of not less than €20,000 per investor for that failure. The European Commission’s own page says the same minimum, and it says the directive does not cover investment risk, such as a share you bought that then falls. A 2010 proposal to raise the minimum was not adopted. Countries may set a higher figure. The client agreement names the legal entity, the scheme and the number. Read that page. This one will not guess it.

Interactive Brokers is a brokerage group used by people who want one account across currencies and markets. The application tells you which legal entity you are contracting with and which regulator watches that entity. A Cyprus address does not, by itself, tell you the answer, and this site will not invent one for 2026. What you can check on the day is the entity named on the agreement, whether client assets are described as segregated, and which compensation scheme, if any, the agreement points to. Segregation is a protection against the firm’s own creditors. It is not a protection against the price of what you bought.

Fees, margin rules, which exchanges you can reach and what interest is paid on idle cash all change. This page does not print them. A stale fee would be a false one. The firm’s own schedule is the source, on the day you look.

Do not read the button as a finding that the account suits you. People who need the money inside a year, or who do not want a balance that can fall, have a simpler answer, which is to leave the surplus in a bank and stop.

Getting the currency there

A broker account denominated in one currency does not remove the cost of arriving in that currency. Salary, an invoice or sale proceeds may start in something other than euros. The conversion, and the fee around it, sit on top of whatever the investment later does. A bank transfer or a licensed money transfer service is one way to move money for that leg. A balance left with a transfer service is not a deposit at a Cyprus credit institution, and the deposit-guarantee scheme above does not apply to it. Read that provider’s own description of how customer funds are held. It can change.

Compare the quote on the day you send, including the spread, not a rate remembered from last quarter. Send a small test if the amount is large enough that a wrong account number would hurt. Keep the receipt. A Cyprus bank that later asks about the source of a transfer is asking a question the bank-account guide already flags. The receipt is the answer, not the brand of the app.

The same point applies in reverse when you bring money back to pay a Cyprus bill. The investment result and the transfer cost are two different numbers. The mortgage comparison is about the cost of borrowing for a home, not about a return on a broker account. Do not set them off against each other in your head as if they were the same decision.

Tax sits beside the account, not inside it

Where the broker is booked does not decide whether you are a Cyprus tax resident, and it does not decide non-dom status. Days, ties and the rules in the tax-residency guide do the first. The non-dom guide does the second. Dividends, interest and gains can be treated differently once those statuses are known. This page does not give you a rate for a foreign dividend or for a fund sale. Publishing one would mean inventing a figure the Tax Department has not put on this site.

What the page will say is narrower. Holding an investment through an overseas broker does not make the income disappear. It also does not, by itself, create a tax you would not have owed on the same income held somewhere else. The facts of residence and of the income come first. An adviser who will put the answer in writing is the person to ask before you move a large surplus on the strength of a summary.

A VPN, if you use one while you log in from a hotel, does not change any of that. The VPN guide is about the network path. Your residency is not an IP address.

A sequence that is still not advice

Write down the amount you might need in the next year and leave it out. That includes a property payment, a tax reserve and a living-cost buffer. You do not need a formula for the buffer. You need a number you would be unhappy to sell investments to raise.

With what remains, decide whether you are prepared for the price to fall and for the money to be unavailable on a bad day in the market. If you are not, a broker account is the wrong tool, including Interactive Brokers. If you are, open the account in your own name, read which entity and which scheme you were given, and only then convert the currency. Keep records of what you sent and what you bought.

Revisit the split when something real changes: a purchase, a sale, a move, a change in how many days you spend in Cyprus. Do not revisit it because a button on a website was confident. Questions

Is Interactive Brokers a savings account?

No. It is a brokerage account. Cash there is not a Cyprus bank deposit, and a fall in the price of what you bought is not covered by investor-compensation rules.

Does the €20,000 minimum cover a falling share price?

No. Directive 97/9/EC sets a minimum for when an investment firm cannot return money or instruments it was holding for you. The European Commission says that minimum does not cover investment risk. Your agreement names the scheme for your entity, and a country may set a higher figure.

Where does a transfer fit if I already have a Cyprus bank?

A bank transfer or a licensed money transfer service is one way to move money between currencies before or after the bank. A balance you leave with a transfer service is not the deposit-guarantee scheme. Compare the quote on the day you send.

Will a broker account change my Cyprus tax?

The country of the broker does not decide tax residency or non-dom status. The same income can still be taxable. This page does not give you a rate.

Should I invest money I need for a property payment this year?

No. Money you expect to send within the year belongs where you can pay it without selling an investment. The calculators are for those tax lines. They are not a reason to put the purchase money into a broker.
