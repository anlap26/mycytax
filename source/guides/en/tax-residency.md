---
title: Cyprus tax residency: the 60-day rule and the 183-day rule
description: How the Tax Department decides that an individual is tax resident in Cyprus, and what that status does and does not do.
---

Tax residence is a yes-or-no question the Income Tax Law answers with two routes. The Tax Department’s current note on Gov.cy says an individual is tax resident if either the 183-day rule or the 60-day rule is met. Everything else people say about “moving to Cyprus” — a yellow slip, a rental contract, a company, a school place — matters only so far as it feeds one of those tests, or a different regime such as the immigration permit.

## The 183-day rule

If you are in Cyprus for more than 183 days in the tax year, you are tax resident. The year is the calendar year. The Department’s page is the place to read how a day is counted. Do not rely on a boarding pass counted by an airline app. Keep a simple record of entries and exits, because a year that lands on 183 rather than 184 is a different answer, and the burden of showing the days is yours if the return is questioned.

Being in Cyprus for 183 days or fewer does not, by itself, make you non-resident. The 60-day rule can still apply. Being abroad for most of the year does not help if the 183-day test is already met.

## The 60-day rule

The Tax Department says you can be tax resident without 183 days if all of the following are true. You are in Cyprus for at least 60 days in the tax year. You do not spend more than 183 days in any other single country. You carry on a business in Cyprus, or you are employed in Cyprus, or you hold an office here, including as a director. And you maintain a permanent home in Cyprus, which you either own or rent.

If the business stops, or the employment or the office ends, during the year, the Department says you are not treated as tax resident under this rule for that year. A directorship that is resigned in March does not carry the rule through to December. The home has to be a real one, owned or rented by you, not a hotel week or a friend’s spare room described as a residence after the event.

PwC’s Tax Facts & Figures 2026 notes that, from 1 January 2026, the older extra condition — that no other state considers you tax resident — no longer applies. The Tax Department’s own page, as it now reads, does not list that condition. It lists the four points above. If you are also treated as resident somewhere else, you may have a double-residence problem and a treaty to read. The 60-day rule’s deletion of that condition does not stop the other country applying its own law.

The 60 days are a minimum, not a target to hit and then leave. The “not more than 183 days in another single country” limb can fail even when the Cyprus days are comfortable, if one other country has the larger share of the year.

## What residence changes

A Cyprus tax resident is within Cyprus income tax on worldwide income, subject to the exemptions the Income Tax Law actually gives, such as the exemption for many foreign dividends and the exemptions for employment income exercised abroad in the cases the law describes. Those exemptions have their own conditions. Residence does not mean every euro is taxed, and non-residence does not mean Cyprus-source income is ignored. Rent from a Cyprus flat, and a gain on Cyprus land, are taxed because of the property, not because of the passport.

From 1 January 2026 the bands on taxable income are nil up to €22,000, 20 per cent to €32,000, 25 per cent to €42,000, 30 per cent to €72,000 and 35 per cent above that. The Tax Department’s older “individuals” page still shows the previous bands, which started at €19,500. The reform pages and PwC’s 2026 facts book are aligned on the new scale. This site uses the new scale and flags the stale page so the conflict is visible.

Special Defence Contribution is a separate tax. It is charged to individuals who are both tax resident and domiciled. Non-dom status is explained on its own page. Residence alone does not create that charge, and the absence of domicile does not remove income tax.

## What residence does not do

It does not give you the right to live in Cyprus. Immigration status is a different file: EU free movement, a visitor stamp, a temporary residence permit, or an immigration permit under Regulation 6(2). A person can be tax resident and still be in the wrong immigration category, and the other way around. A 60-day plan that depends on a job or a directorship also fails if that tie is cancelled. Build the tie first, then count the days.

It does not, by itself, make you non-resident anywhere else. If you keep a home, a family or a business in another country, that country’s rules still apply to you. Cyprus will not write to that country to resign on your behalf.

It is not the same as the 5 per cent VAT test. VAT asks whether the dwelling is your primary and permanent residence and whether the area and value limits are met. Tax residence asks where you spend the year and whether the statutory ties exist. You can be tax resident and fail the VAT test, because the flat is a second home. You can pass the VAT test in a year when you are not yet tax resident. Do not let one application’s wording be copied into the other.

## A practical record

Keep the lease or the title, the employment contract or the register of directors, and a day count you could show to the Tax Department. If you will use the 60-day rule, check in the autumn whether any single other country is about to go through 183 days, and whether the Cyprus employment or office is still in place. A rule that is failed in November cannot be repaired by a long stay in December if the other-country limb is already broken, and it cannot be repaired at all if the employment has already ended.

The Department’s form and the annual return are the formal steps. This page is the map of the statute, not the return.

## FAQ

### Is 60 days enough on its own?

No. The Tax Department requires the 60 days, the limit of 183 days in any other one country, a business, employment or office in Cyprus that is not terminated during the year, and a permanent home you own or rent.

### Did the “not tax resident elsewhere” test disappear?

PwC’s 2026 summary says that condition no longer applies from 1 January 2026, and the Tax Department’s current page does not list it. Another country’s own residence rules can still apply to you.

### Does tax residence give me 5 per cent VAT?

No. The VAT relief has its own conditions: primary and permanent residence, and the area and value caps. Tax residence is neither necessary nor sufficient for that relief.
