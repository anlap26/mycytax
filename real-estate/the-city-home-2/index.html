The City Home 2 is a project of 12 homes by ATEX Developers Ltd in Geroskipou, Paphos. Ten of the twelve homes are available. Each home has its own title deed. The energy rating is A, and there are no annual communal fees. Homes have 2 or 3 bedrooms. Type 1 is a detached 3-bedroom villa of about 170–175 m². Type 2 is a semi-detached 2-bedroom home of about 148–152 m². Plots are about 243–286 m². Covered area is from about 148 m². Prices exclude VAT and are a special offer: from €715,000 +VAT for a 2-bedroom semi-detached home, and from €790,100 +VAT for a 3-bedroom villa, up to €844,000 +VAT. The price on this page is the starting figure, from €715,000 +VAT.
