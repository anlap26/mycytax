Sources

On a resale, transfer fees follow the Department of Lands and Surveys scale, 3% to €85,000, 5% to €170,000 and 8% above, then the 50% reduction unless the seller took the property in a forced sale under Parts VI and VIA of Law 9/1965. A contract not deposited within six months can add 10%, on the Department’s leaflet. Gov.cy says transfer fees are not charged when the transfer is a VAT transaction, so the new-build choice sets them to zero and charges VAT instead: 19%, or 5% on the qualifying portion of a primary residence within the area and value limits used on the VAT calculator. Stamp duty is €0 when at least one party signs on or after 1 January 2026, under Law 239(I)/2025. The old capped scale is used only if a party signed by 31 December 2025. Legal fees, the agent and local charges are not in the total.
