Sources

Cyprus personal tax uses the 2026 bands on Business in Cyprus. Portugal uses the 2026 marginal rates in Decreto-Lei n.º 212/2026, on rendimento coletável, with no deductions. The UAE personal line is nil, as the official government portal states that the UAE does not levy income tax on individuals (page dated 28 October 2026). A rate you type is your figure, not a rate this site has checked. Company rates are headlines only: Cyprus 15% from the Tax Department’s 2026 table; Malta 35% on the Malta Tax and Customs Administration corporate page, last updated 8 October 2025, with no shareholder refund applied; Portugal 19% for 2026 on the mainland under Lei n.º 64/2025 and the Portal das Finanças 2026 rate card; Greece 22% for legal persons from tax year 2021 onwards, on the Ministry of Economy and Finance income-tax guide; UAE 9%, and the 0% band up to AED 375,000 is applied only to the euro amount you type, because this page does not publish an exchange rate. Malta’s 2026 personal bands and the child-dependent middle bands of the Greek 2026 employment scale are not calculated. Social insurance is not included.
