Stamp duty on a purchase contract

Stamp duty is €0 on a contract signed from 1 January 2026. The old scale still applies if a party signed by 31 December 2025.

VAT on a new property

Standard VAT is 19%. The 5% rate covers only part of a qualifying primary residence, within the area and value limits.

Capital gains tax

A 20% tax on the inflation-adjusted gain, with the lifetime exemptions in the Capital Gains Tax Law as amended for 2026.
