Mortgage payment

Monthly payment on a euro loan at a fixed rate. The rate is the one your bank offers. This page does not publish a market rate.

Sources

The monthly payment is the standard annuity: the loan, the monthly interest rate and the number of months. It assumes the rate never changes and that you pay no bank fee, no life-policy premium and no early-repayment charge. Cyprus banks publish their own rates. This page deliberately does not state one.
